Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026
Android application: com.zehna.jorlio

Zehna Tech provides Jorlio PDF, an Android app for creating, editing, merging and exporting PDFs. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Documents stay on your device

Jorlio PDF processes your documents on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your PDFs, images, text, signatures or project contents. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics or third-party crash-reporting services.

Information the app accesses and uses

The app accesses files you choose through Android's file picker, including PDFs, images and text files. It uses their contents and file names to import, display, edit and export your documents. If you add text or a signature, that content becomes part of your local project and may be included in a PDF you export.

The app stores copies of imported content, project names, edits, document previews and generated PDFs in its app storage. It also stores your theme preference on the device. These files and preferences support the features you choose to use; they are not sent to Zehna Tech.

Saving and sharing

When you choose Save or Share, Android lets you select a destination or another app. The PDF is provided to that destination at your request. A destination may be a cloud storage service or another third party, whose own privacy policy governs its handling of the file. Using a cloud-backed source in the file picker may also involve that provider.

We do not sell your information or automatically upload or share your documents. Temporary copies of PDFs may remain in the app's cache after sharing so the receiving app can read them.

Storage, security and deletion

Projects remain in app storage until you delete them or remove the app's data. You can delete a project from its menu on the home screen; this removes its saved project files and associated assets and exports from the project folder. Import and sharing caches may remain separately until Android clears them or you clear the app's cache or storage.

You can use Android Settings to clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove its local app data. Files you saved outside the app, original source files, and copies shared with another app are separate and must be deleted from those locations or services.

Jorlio PDF uses Android's app storage protections and disables Android automatic backup for its app data. It does not add its own encryption layer to project storage; protect your device with a screen lock and keep it updated. No storage or security method can guarantee absolute protection.

Privacy inquiries

If you contact us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and retain the correspondence only as needed to handle it or meet applicable legal obligations. Please avoid sending sensitive documents unless they are necessary for your inquiry. You can request deletion of your correspondence by contacting us.
